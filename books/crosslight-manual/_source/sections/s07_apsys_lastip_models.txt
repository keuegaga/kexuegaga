ThemodelsinSection14.2werebasedontheGreen’sfunctionapproachandmuch

The task is to start from a continuous dipole source as a function of wavelength

RCLED power emission strongly depends on the overlap of the optical field with
the dipole source. Existence of the dipole source does not necessarily mean ra-
diative recombination will occur. For example, the simple term Bn2 only means
that if carriers exist in the active layer of a device, a dipole source is available
there. It does not guarantee the generation of electron-hole pairs. For that to
happen, the optical field should exist so that the dipole moment (displacement
times the field) can be formed to complete the energy conversion from current
to photon. Therefore, in RCLED model, the simple term Bn2 (and other form of
QWrecombinationsource)mustbescaledbyaninterferenceenhancementfactor
