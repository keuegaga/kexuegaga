What is Suprem4?
 Stanford’s 2D code 1970s to 1990s (up to 1 
um technology).
 Intension:  to cover process steps from 
substrate all the way to full device structure.
 Deposit, etch, implant, diffusion, oxidation, 
stress analysis.
 Core model: dopant diffusion (point defect 
model).
 Intended to interface to Pisces-2B (Stanford 
device 2D simulator).

===== PDF page 4 =====
4
What is Csuprem? 
 Extension of technology to below 1 um (low energy 
implant models) while maintaining full compatibility.
 Extension of Stanford’s code to full 3D with inter-plane 
coupling.
 Direct use of existing 2D input decks in 3D simulation.
 Full 3D model for implantation, diffusion, segregation & 
oxidation.
 Direct conversion of GDSII file into 3D simulation input 
decks.
 Full 3D simulation for mechanical stress,  bending and  
vibration motions.
 Flexibility of switching between quasi-3D and full-3D 
modes according to speed/accuracy requirements. 
Extension of Stanford’s code to full 3D with inter-plane 
coupling.

APSYS for MOSFET
 Temperature dependent impact ionization.
 Quantum mechanical models for carrier 
confinement and tunneling.
 Various vertical field dependent mobility 
models.
 Self-heating/thermal analysis with various 
types of thermal boundaries.
 Convenient transient/AC analysis suitable for 
various types of floating gate simulation.
 Parallel computing capability for unlimited 
mesh and multi-core CPU, suitable for
